Research Decision Cards: ASTRA's filtered decision layer. It combines entry quality, final score, context, risk/reward and gate status.
Unified Opportunity Board: a broader comparison board combining multiple engines/sources on one scale. It is useful for discovery and comparison, but a high rank there does not automatically mean the setup passed the V5.7 decision gate.
